Our Story

I'm Aruna Krishna, a dentist in Seattle, practice owner, entrepreneur, and proud mom of two girls. The idea for Vaani began during a family trip to India.

I was getting ready for a family get-together and had chosen a beautiful boat-neck dress. As I looked in the mirror, I realized my bra straps were showing. I didn't have the right bra for the neckline and was about to change my outfit when my sister handed me a pair of decorative bra straps and said, "Try these."

The moment I put them on, I fell in love.

Not only did they solve my problem, they transformed something I had always tried to hide into something beautiful enough to be seen. I loved how elegant and comfortable they were, and how they added a touch of style without trying too hard. They elevated my outfit effortlessly and completely changed the way I thought about bra straps.

I bought several pairs and gifted them to my cousins in India. I brought a few back to Seattle and later shared them with my office team.

Every time I wore them, I was reminded that something so practical could also be beautiful. I kept wondering,

"Why should bra straps always be hidden? Why can't they become part of our style?"

Life became busy with my dental practice and family, and the idea of starting something new moved to the bottom of my list. But the dream never left me. I kept thinking about those beautiful straps and imagining how I could introduce them to women in the United States.

In early 2026, I finally decided it was time to turn that dream into reality.

As I researched these beautiful straps, I discovered they were handcrafted by talented women artisans in a small village in South India. Learning the story behind their craftsmanship made me appreciate them even more. I was inspired not only by the beauty of their work but also by the opportunity to support the women behind them.

I partnered with these talented artisans to create collections thoughtfully curated especially for women in the United States, blending traditional artistry with modern fashion while preserving the authenticity and beauty of every handcrafted piece.

And that's how Vaani was born.

Vaani is more than a fashion accessory.

  • It's about confidence.
  • It's about celebrating artistry.
  • It's about empowering women.
  • It's about turning something ordinary into something beautiful.

At Vaani, we believe bra straps don't have to be hidden. They're designed to be seen.

Today, I have the privilege of pursuing two passions. Dentistry is my craft, and Vaani is my art. Together, they reflect my love for precision, creativity, the artistry of skilled hands, confidence, and style. I feel truly grateful and honored to be able to do both.

Thank you for supporting my dream, the talented artisans behind every pair, and a brand that celebrates confidence, individuality, and timeless style.

With gratitude,
Aruna Krishna
Founder, Vaani
